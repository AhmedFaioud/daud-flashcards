How to install

  1. Install Anki — free on desktop and Android. On iPhone the app is AnkiMobile, which is paid (about €25) — the deck itself is free.
  2. Open the downloaded file. On a computer, double-click it. On a phone, download it first, then tap it and choose “Open in Anki”.
  3. Study 10 minutes a day. The deck schedules itself: cards you know come back rarely, cards you miss come back tomorrow.
If nothing happens on iPhone: open the Files app, find the .apkg, tap it, and choose Share → AnkiMobile.
